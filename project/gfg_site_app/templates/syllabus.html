StudySoft Syllabus

Find below our current syllabus.
It does not yet include all parts of the KS3 syllabus, but we're working to add more topics!

1. Algebra

1.1 Linear equations with unknowns on both sides
1.2 Solving equations with brackets
1.3 Finding the nth term of an arithmetic progression
1.4 Finding the arithmetic common difference (including negatives)
1.5 Finding missing terms


2. Area and Perimeter

2.1 Area and perimeter of rectangles
2.2 Area and perimeter of triangles
2.3 Area and perimeter of trapezia and parallelograms
2.4 Area and circumference of circles and semicircles, using π, 3.14 or 22/7
2.5 Enlargement and scale factor of area
2.6 Percentage change in area


3. Fractions, Decimals and Percentages

3.1 Converting between fractions, decimals and percentages
3.2 Rounding (decimal places)
3.3 Percentage increase and decrease
3.4 Mixed-form problem solving


4. Probability and Statistics

4.1 Measures of central tendency: Mean median and mode
4.2 Measures of regression: range, variance and standard deviation.
4.3 Missing values from a given mean or median
4.4 Finding the most representative average (outliers)
4.5 Effect of scaling data on mean and range


5. Ratio and Proportion

5.1 Simplifying ratios (including decimals)
5.2 Sharing items according to a ratio
5.3 Best value comparisons
5.4 Direct proportion and the unitary method
5.5 Real world applications: map scales, currency conversion, time estimates
