Privacy Policy

Summary

At StudySoft we must collect certain information from our users for our site to run. In this policy, we explain what we collect and why.
We collect only what is necessary for you to use our site. Your data is held securely, and can be deleted when you request this or when you have not accessed the site in a long time.

Introduction

StudySoft is a maths practice site for Key Stage 3 (Year 7 to 9) students (ages 11 to 14).
We take privacy seriously and aim to both meet and exceed out legal obligations. This policy is inteded to be easily comprehensible for the benefit of our users. The policy applies ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) standards, including the ICO Children's Code for Edtech products.
StudySoft is the data controller for this purposes, and our hosting provider is the processor.

Data We Collect

We use basic session and CSRF cookies to keey users logged in and keep the site secure.
We do not collect user's full names, home addresses or photographs. On registration, we also ask users to not use their real names as their usernames We collect an email address for each user solely for account recovery and the delivery of key messages.

How We Use Your Data

  • To run your account and track your progress
  • To keep you logged in during study sessions and keep your account secure
  • To keep the site secure, and investigate and fix complaints

Legal Basis

Under UK GDPR (Article 6), Our primary legal basis of data collection is to provide the service we have contracted to provide our users.
We also rely on the basis of consent, meaning consent of our users over age 13, and consent of the parents of our users under age 13.

Data Retention

We retain our users' data while they are using our services. We delete this data after 12 months of inactivity of 2 months after a valid deletion request by a user.
We store data in hashed format, with secure HTTPS connections.

Your Rights

You may request to view, correct or delete your data on our systems at any time. You can also withdraw consent for the use of your data at any time and this will mean you are no longer able to use our service. Contact us at privacy@study-soft.co.uk for these requests.
Users aged 13 and over can make these requests by themselves. Parents and guardians can also make these requests on behalf of users, particularly users aged below 13.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Policy Updates

This policy was last updated on 21st September 2026. Updates will continue as our policies evolve. The next planned review is on 20th September 2027.
