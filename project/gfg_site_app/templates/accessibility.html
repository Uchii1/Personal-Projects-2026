Accessibility Statement

Summary

In this policy, we explain what we have done to ensure our web application can be used effectively by everyone.
We also explain how to address complaints regarding accessibility.

Introduction

StudySoft is a maths practice site for Key Stage 3 (Year 7 to 9) students (ages 11 to 14).
We want every student to be able to use it confidently, including those with disabilities or additional needs.

Our Standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.
We believe the site is presently partially conformant.

Compatibility and Testing

The site is built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python and Django technologies. It has been tested with Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox web browsers, which are the recommended browsers for its use. Accessibility testing has been completed with WAVE.
To use the site effectively, a desktop or laptop computer is recommended, alongside a mouse or touchscreen. This will help students view text comfortably and select quiz answers.
It is assumed that students have a calculator and writing materials at hand when attempting the questions.
The site has also been developed with tablet and phone screens in mind, where answers to quizzes can be selected via touchscreen. However, a larger screen is ideal.

What We Have Done to Make StudySoft Accessible

  • Readable text: We mainly use black or dark purple text on a cream background. The cream background offers a strong contrast to the text, while being easier on the eyes.
    We have avoided italics, except when particularly useful. We have also applied dyslexia-friendly sans serif fonts in the font options, and we have used a minimum font size of 12.

  • Uncluttered pages: The site avoids decorative graphics. The quiz questions and solutions only use images where necessary.

  • Alt text: All images that we have used also include relevant and helpful descriptors in the alt text, to help our users that require screen readers.

  • Study tools: An optional Pomodoro timer with a sound alarm helps students pace their work. The timer opens in a new tab, so the user's progress on the quiz is not lost.
    There is also a formula page with geometric formulae.

  • Break resources: Short games (mental maths and number guessing) are included, which can be used during study breaks.

  • Progress charts: Explanations are added below progress charts, to ensure they are understandable to younger users.

Known Limitations

Some interactive questions and the games may not yet work fully with screen readers or keyboard-only navigation.
The progress graphics, question graphics and pomodoro timer may not meet all WCAG criteria. We are working to improve these areas by 20th September 2027.

Feedback and Support

If you find a barrier or need content in another format, such as a printable worksheet for a specific quiz, please contact us at hello@study-soft.co.uk. We aim to reply within seven UK working days.

Preparation of this Statement

This statement was prepared on 20th September 2026 and was last reviewed on 16th October 2026.
It is based on assessment by a trained IT professional and automated testing with WAVE.
